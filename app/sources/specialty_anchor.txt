Oral and maxillofacial surgery: clinical research relevant to a practising maxillofacial surgeon. Orthognathic surgery and virtual surgical planning for dentofacial deformities; maxillofacial trauma including mandibular, midface, orbital and zygomatic fractures; temporomandibular joint disorders, arthroscopy and joint replacement; cleft lip and palate and craniofacial anomalies; oral cavity, jaw and salivary gland tumours, neck dissection and free-flap microvascular reconstruction; dentoalveolar surgery and third molar extraction; dental implants, bone grafting, sinus augmentation and alveolar ridge reconstruction; medication-related osteonecrosis of the jaw and osteoradionecrosis; odontogenic infections and cysts; facial aesthetic surgery; anaesthesia, sedation, analgesia and perioperative care in maxillofacial patients; complications, outcomes and patient-reported quality of life after these procedures.
