Tell Me Kort recommends research papers to surgeons. This page says what it keeps about you, who else handles it, and how to remove it.

What we keep

  • Your email address and name, to sign you in and send your weekly email.
  • Your research interests: the topics you confirm, seed papers, weights and minimums. The free text you type when setting up is used once to suggest topics and is not stored.
  • Your feedback: likes, papers marked read or not for you, and scores you dispute.
  • When weekly emails were sent and which papers they contained.
  • Sign-in requests (your email and the time), deleted after an hour.

No patient data is collected or stored, ever. Please don't put any in a comment or in your interests.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking pixels. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

Who else handles it

  • Render hosts the app and its database in Frankfurt, in the EU.
  • {{ email_provider }} delivers our emails, so it receives your email address and the email's contents. We add no open or click tracking.
  • Anthropic grades papers and turns your interests into topic suggestions, so it receives paper text and the interests you type during setup.
  • Voyage AI turns paper text and your topic descriptions into vectors for matching.

Anthropic, Voyage AI and {{ email_provider }} may process this data outside the EU under their own data processing terms. Paper data comes from PubMed (NCBI); nothing about you is sent there.

How long, and how to remove it

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time from Settings: that removes everything above straight away. Papers and their grades are shared by all users and stay.
